## 1. Algorithmic & Scientific Limitations

### 1.1 Anomaly Score Interpretation & Calibration
* **Metric Nature:** The anomaly score produced by the primary PatchCore and PaDiM backbones represents the minimum Euclidean or Mahalanobis distance to nominal feature representations in embedding space. It is **not** a calibrated posterior probability of defect existence ($P(y=1|x)$).
* **Per-Category Variation:** Baseline nominal distances vary naturally across material categories (e.g., $1.22$ for smooth glass bottles vs $2.18$ for stranded electrical cables). Consequently, a universal numerical threshold cannot be applied globally across different manufacturing lines without per-category calibration.
* **Mitigation:** The system provides per-category baseline statistics (`step9_severity_baselines.csv`) and allows operators to adjust decision thresholds interactively via UI controls.

### 1.2 Operational Severity Index Heuristic
* **Non-Physical Metric:** The severity index ($0–100$) is an operational triage heuristic formulated as:
  $$\text{Severity} = w_1 \cdot \text{Normalized Peak Distance} + w_2 \cdot \text{Defect Surface Area \%}$$
* **Limitation:** It does **not** reflect physical defect volume, 3D crack depth, material yield stress, or structural failure probability under tensile load.
* **Mitigation:** The UI explicitly labels the severity index as an *"Operational Triage Heuristic (Not Physical Ground Truth)"*.

### 1.3 LLM & RAG Root-Cause Recommendations
* **Correlation vs Causation:** The RAG Defect Copilot queries a curated database of manufacturing engineering rules (`defect_knowledge_base.csv`). Suggestions generated by the LLM (e.g. diagnosing a blister defect as "mold overheating") are probabilistic correlations based on historical engineering literature, not certified physical root-cause analyses (RCA).
* **Mitigation:** All conversational outputs include clear disclaimers requiring validation by certified plant quality personnel before initiating tooling or line modifications.

## 2. Hardware & Performance Constraints

### 2.1 Inference Latency on CPU vs GPU
* **GPU (NVIDIA RTX 3080/4090):** Mean inference latency is approximately $18\text{–}25\text{ ms}$ per image, supporting real-time conveyor speeds up to $40\text{–}50\text{ fps}$.
* **CPU (Intel Core i7/Xeon):** Mean latency increases to $120\text{–}260\text{ ms}$ per image due to high-dimensional feature extraction across WideResNet-50 intermediate layers and FAISS nearest-neighbor searches. CPU execution is suitable for batch verification and inline stations operating under $4\text{ parts/second}$, but cannot support high-speed continuous web inspection without hardware acceleration.
* **Mitigation:** The UI provides asynchronous background batch processing and progress polling to prevent blocking the operator interface.

### 2.2 Memory Footprint for Multi-Line Deployment
* **RAM Allocation:** Hosting memory banks across all 15 industrial categories concurrently requires approximately $1.46\text{ GB}$ of resident RAM.
* **Scaling Limit:** For facilities with $>50$ concurrent product variants, dynamic memory-bank page swapping or product-specific worker processes must be deployed.

## 3. Optical & Presentation Boundary Conditions

### 3.1 Part Alignment & Lighting Sensitivity
* **Optical Reticle Constraint:** The system assumes standardized industrial camera presentation with relatively fixed object alignment and consistent illumination.
* **Sensitivity to Drastic Lighting Changes:** Severe uncontrolled lighting shifts ($\Delta\text{Lux} > 40\%$) or direct specular glare can trigger false positive anomaly flags on surface textures (e.g. carpet or wood).
* **Mitigation:** The integrated Drift Monitoring Dashboard (`DriftSection.jsx`) alerts operators when distribution shifts exceed statistical control limits.

### 3.2 Complex Conveyor Clutter & Multiple Unfixtured Parts
* **Single-Part Assumption:** PatchCore and PaDiM are designed for single-specimen inspection within a defined field of view.
* **Experimental Reticle Demarcation:** While a preliminary YOLOv8 optical crop reticle was prototyped (Notebook Cells 41–42), it is classified as an **Experimental Prototype (P3)**. In production, physical mechanical fixturing or calibrated conveyor photo-eyes should be used to trigger image capture.

## 4. Quarantined Research Components

### 4.1 External Chukyo Dataset Generalization
* **Quarantined Status:** The external Chukyo automotive stamping dataset remains strictly in cold storage. No inference, threshold tuning, or hyperparameter optimization has been conducted on this dataset.
* **Constraint:** Any claims of cross-domain transferability to unseen external manufacturing plants remain open scientific hypotheses to be validated under the protocol specified in `research/EXPERIMENT_MATRIX.md`.
