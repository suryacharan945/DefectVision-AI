## 1. Resolution of the Eight Core Threshold Questions

### Question 1: Where did all 30 nominal images originate?
- **20 Images:** Sourced from MVTec AD benchmark test set: `DefectVisionAI/dataset/raw/mvtec/bottle/test/good/` (`000.png` through `019.png`).
- **10 Images:** Sourced from simulated Station 01 validation normal set: `data/station_01/val_normal/` (`station01_normal_val_000.png` through `009.png`).
- **Zero Images:** Originated from the target external studio domain (`Domain B: Controlled External Photos`).

### Question 2: Which partitions do they belong to?
- **The 20 MVTec Images:** Belong strictly to the `FINAL_TEST` partition of the MVTec AD benchmark.
- **The 10 Station 01 Images:** Belong to `NORMAL_CALIBRATION` (indices 000–004) and `VALIDATION` (indices 005–009) of the Station 01 dataset.
- **Independence from `bottle_sku_btl_adapt_002` Training/Calibration:** NONE of these 30 images were used in the memory bank ($N=5$) or threshold calibration ($N=2$) of `bottle_sku_btl_adapt_002`.

### Question 3: Were any of these 30 images used to select thresholds?
- **No.** The active provisional threshold ($1.6410$) was computed strictly from $N=2$ calibration frames during profile registration in `test_adaptation_and_intake.py`. The 30 nominal images were evaluated post-hoc in `scripts/audit_threshold_calibration.py` as an empirical audit set.

### Question 4: Are the 30 images physically independent specimens?
- **MVTec Subset ($N=20$):** Yes. The 20 MVTec bottle test images represent physically distinct glass bottles photographed under standardized laboratory lighting.
- **Station 01 Subset ($N=10$):** **No.** As established in the dataset lineage audit, all 10 Station 01 images are synthetic OpenCV affine, perspective, and brightness jitter variations of a **single physical bottle specimen** (`PHYS_SOURCE_MVTEC_BOTTLE_NOMINAL_JITTER`).

### Question 5: Are any of them augmented versions of other images?
- **Yes.** All 10 images in `val_normal/` are algorithmic jitter augmentations generated by `scripts/prepare_station_01_data.py`. They cannot be treated as independent physical draws from a production line.

### Question 6: How was the bootstrap confidence interval calculated?
- In `scripts/audit_threshold_calibration.py` (lines 103–111):
  ```python
  rng = np.random.RandomState(42)
  boot_p99 = []
  for _ in range(1000):
      sample = rng.choice(arr, size=len(arr), replace=True)
      boot_p99.append(np.percentile(sample, 99))
  ci_low = float(np.percentile(boot_p99, 2.5))
  ci_high = float(np.percentile(boot_p99, 97.5))
  ```
  The interval $[1.9315, 3.0528]$ was obtained via 1,000 bootstrap resamples ($N=30$ with replacement) of the 30 cross-domain audit scores.

### Question 7: Why did the report refer to $N=2$ while presenting a 30-image distribution?
- **Conflation of Profile Calibration vs. Audit Evaluation:**
  - The profile's **active operating threshold** ($\tau = 1.6410$) was derived from exactly $N=2$ calibration frames (`station01_normal_train_003` and `004`).
  - The **30-image distribution** was an independent audit dataset introduced to evaluate how that provisional threshold performs across broader nominal variations.
  - The previous report correctly criticized $N=2$ as having zero statistical power, but improperly conflated the $N=2$ profile calibration sample size with the bootstrap uncertainty of the 30-image audit set.

### Question 8: Were reported FPR values measured on independent data or estimated from threshold-selection data?
- The reported **63.3% False Positive Rate (FPR)** was measured on the 30 cross-domain audit images (which were independent of threshold selection).
- **Critical Flaw in Prior Interpretation:** The 63.3% FPR is a **cross-domain mismatch rejection rate**, NOT an in-domain false positive rate! Because `bottle_sku_btl_adapt_002` was trained on external studio lighting (`Domain B`), presenting it with MVTec benchmark bottles (`Domain A`) and Station 01 conveyor images (`Domain D`) represents domain-shifted input. 19 out of 30 cross-domain normal bottles scored $\ge 1.6410$.
- In the actual target domain (`Domain B`), there are only 2 normal images (`clear bottle.jpg` and `clear bottle2.webp`), both of which were placed in the memory bank (scoring 1.39 and 1.38). Thus, true held-out in-domain FPR has never been measured because no independent target-domain nominal bottles currently exist.

## 5. Statistical Requirements & New Data-Collection Protocol

### Why Existing Data Cannot Support Guaranteed Calibration
1. **Sample Size Deficit:** By the fundamental law of order statistics, non-parametrically bounding a 99th percentile false alarm rate ($\text{FAR} \le 1\%$) requires a minimum sample size of $N \ge 100$ independent nominal frames ($p \approx 1 - 1/N$).
2. **Domain Incoherence:** Calibrating `bottle_sku_btl_adapt_002` on synthetic Station 01 images while evaluating it on external studio or MVTec images violates the identically distributed assumption ($i.i.d.$).
3. **No Mathematical Shortcut:** Extreme Value Theory (EVT / Weibull tail fitting) cannot circumvent sample size deficits. Fitting Weibull parameters on $N=2$ or $N=5$ points yields wide parameter covariance that merely masks uncertainty with statistical formality.

### Prescribed Protocol for Production Calibration
To transition `bottle_sku_btl_adapt_002` from **PROVISIONAL** to **CERTIFIED INDUSTRIAL CALIBRATION**, the following data collection protocol is mandatory:
1. **Target Camera Rig Fixed:** Collect images exclusively from `CAM-EXT-01` with locked aperture, exposure time, focal length, and lighting diffuser.
2. **$N \ge 50$ Physical Nominal Specimens:** Capture at least 50 physically distinct, defect-free 500ml clear glass bottles.
3. **Multi-Session Acquisition:** Partition into 3 separate sessions across morning, afternoon, and night shifts to capture ambient thermal and ambient light drift.
4. **Partition Isolation:**
   - 10 specimens $\rightarrow$ Memory Bank Training (`MEMORY_BANK_TRAIN`)
   - 20 specimens $\rightarrow$ Calibrated Threshold Determination (`NORMAL_CALIBRATION`)
   - 20 specimens $\rightarrow$ Strict Held-Out Operational Verification (`FINAL_TEST`)
5. **Threshold Rule:** $\tau = \text{quantile}_{0.99}(S_{\text{calib}})$. Verify that $S_{\text{test}}$ produces $\le 1$ false alarm across the 20 held-out test frames.
